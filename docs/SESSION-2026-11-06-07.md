## 1. What the audit got right, and what it could not see

The audit was A-class: it separated claimed from evidenced, re-derived every number
arithmetically, and named contradictions between documents. Its findings were correct on
drift (stale README, a dead TODO ledger, `--max-age 60` documented as 45) and correct that a
gate had been forgotten on one lane.

It could not see code, so its two most important questions were open. Both were answered from
the source tree, and both were worse than the audit had reason to hope:

- **"Does the escrow check live below the env layer?"** It did not. The rule existed in
  exactly one function, `bounty_admission.admit()`, which the hydration drain never calls.
- **"The claim was never made — why?"** Because the claim was written into the PR body and
  `post_claim` was never called for a newly created PR, and because the scheduler's own engine
  has a publication path with no claim at all.

## 4. The decision Baba asked for, and why

**Owner-allocated funding is now a second escrow class** (`14937b8`, 84 tests).

The escrow-only gate accepted only a platform bot's attestation, which excluded
`fyne-io/fyne#1062` — the largest verified money in the record. andydotxyz, the repository
owner, wrote "$500 is currently allocated for eligible contributors completing this work", then
"the bounty on this ticket has increased to **$1000**!", then allocated half of it to work he
accepts. No bot attests it, so it was refused as `NO_PLATFORM_ESCROW`.

The bot-only rule rested on a 0%-conversion measurement for non-escrow offers. That measurement
was taken on our own interference: **46 of our 50 closed PRs were closed by our own account, 37
of them within six hours of creation.** The 0% describes what we did to our own PRs, not what
the market did. Meanwhile the bot pool measures 59 tickets of which none are both fresh and
unswamped.

The new class is a fact gate, not a judgement: the funder is the issue's author, that author's
association is OWNER, MEMBER or COLLABORORATE, and a specific amount is named. A stranger's
promise is still refused. Stated limitation: a MEMBER committing money on a stranger's ticket
is not admitted, because the association read is the issue author's.

## 8. Scheduler state

The laptop daemon (`autonomous_icu_pipeline.py`) works: cycle #1 ran, delivery DB
`integrity=ok rows=369`, discovery across 39 queries, `bounty in:title` → 10 raw → 1 retained.
It has terminal-state memory (`FAILED_TERMINAL`, `REJECTED_SECURITY`) so it does not
re-dispatch dead work.

It cannot dispatch: `FLEET_DOOR_TOKEN` is unset in its environment, so the relay answers 403.
Its interval is already aggressive — 180 seconds normally, **30 seconds** whenever the fleet
queue holds fewer than two tasks — which is the lever the market measurement points at, since
the winnable tickets are the ones that open today.

GitHub Actions cron (`:07/:37` discovery, `:22/:52` hydration) is still what actually runs.
Retiring it was deferred deliberately: it is only safe to retire the cron once the laptop path
dispatches, and nothing is gained by removing the working scheduler to make the migration look
finished.
